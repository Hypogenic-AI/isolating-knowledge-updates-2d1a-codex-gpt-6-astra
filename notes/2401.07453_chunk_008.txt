A.4.3 Downstream Evaluation
In this section, we show plots for the downstream
evaluations for both GPT2-XL (1.5B) and GPT-J
(6B) for the four samples. Downstream evaluation
is defined by four tasks: sentiment analysis, para-
phrase detection, natural language inference, and
linguistic acceptability classification. Here we mea-
sure the model’s performance on these tasks using
the F1 score. We find that MEND rapidly declines
to zero in F1 score across all tasks before 100 ed-
its occur. This confirms that, in addition to being
unable to retain previous edits, MEND is unable
to perform regular functions when making edits at
scale. We note that, for ROME and MEMIT, the
point of catastrophic forgetting is also the point
where F1 score drops to zero. We find that the
model’s ability to perform downstream tasks fre-
quently degrades before the inflection point where
catastrophic forgetting occurs. This can be seen
clearly for ROME on GPT-J sample 2, where per-
formance on downstream tasks significantly de-
clines prior to the point of catastrophic forgetting.
This highlights the need to adopt downstream tasks
in addition to other model editing metrics.
