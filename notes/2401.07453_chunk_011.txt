A.6.4 FT
The Fine-Tuning procedures used in this paper fol-
low from (Meng et al., 2022b) and (Meng et al.,
2022a)’s implementation for both GPT-J and GPT2-
XL. MLP weights for a single layer are fine-tuned
for both models. We use a constrained fine tuning
approach where we add aL∞ constraint such that
∥θG−θG′∥∞≤ ϵ at each gradient step. For the
constraint,ϵ = 5e−4 for GPT2-XL andϵ = 5e−5
for GPT-J. It is optimized using Adam with a learn-
ing rate of 5e-4 for both GPT2-XL and GPT-J. We
fine tune layers 18 and 6 for GPT2-XL and GPT-J
respectively.
